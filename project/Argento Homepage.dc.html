Why packed to spec

Not all pre-packed columns are created equal.

Results vary with resin, operator experience, and technique — and a badly packed column means a re-pack at your expense, on top of the time already lost. We pack to your exact specification and test every column before it ships.

Wasted time

Columns arrive ready to use, not waiting on two to three hours of packing and testing.

Variable results

Packed by experienced technicians to consistent spec, project to project.

Re-packing risk

Outsourced to us — a bad pack is our problem to fix, not yours.

Hardware waste

Reuse what you own through Column Swap instead of buying new hardware every time.

Solvent safety

The overflow you never see coming.

A waste carboy fills quietly, overnight, while nobody is watching the bench. Our solvent safety line closes that gap — monitoring the container, capturing the vapour, and keeping the whole path sealed from column outlet to waste.

Polypropylene and PTFE throughout, built for continuous solvent service and specified to fit the fittings you already run.
